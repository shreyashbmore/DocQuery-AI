# Sample Academic Regulations (fictional, for testing DocQuery AI)

## 1. Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in every course to be eligible for the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65 and 75 percent may apply for condonation on medical grounds with a certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner. Students below 65 percent attendance are detained in that course and must repeat it.

## 2. Credits and Grading

A Bachelor of Engineering degree requires a total of 160 credits over eight semesters. Each theory course carries 3 or 4 credits and each laboratory course carries 1 credit. Grades are awarded on a ten-point scale, where O is 10, A+ is 9, A is 8, B+ is 7, B is 6, C is 5 and P is 4. A grade of F means the course must be cleared in a supplementary examination.

## 3. Internal Assessment

Internal assessment carries 40 marks and the end-semester examination carries 60 marks. Internal assessment consists of two class tests of 15 marks each and 10 marks for assignments. A student must score at least 16 out of 40 in internal assessment to pass a course.

## 4. Final Year Project

The final year project is carried out in groups of at most four students under a faculty guide. The project carries 12 credits spread across the seventh and eighth semesters. A mid-term review is held in week 8 of the seventh semester and the final viva voce is conducted by an external examiner at the end of the eighth semester.

## 5. Academic Integrity

Plagiarism in assignments, reports or projects is a serious offence. A first offence results in zero marks for that component. A repeated offence is referred to the Academic Integrity Committee, which may cancel the semester registration.
